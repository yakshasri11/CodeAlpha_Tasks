Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email, username, profile photo, bio, and content you post. We also collect usage data such as interactions, session times, and device information.

Community Guidelines

Be Kind & Respectful

Synvora is a space for creativity and authentic connection. Treat everyone with respect, even when you disagree. Healthy debate is welcome — harassment is not.

Share Responsibly

Only post content you own or have rights to share. Credit creators when reposting. Do not spread misinformation or manipulated media.

Keep It Safe

  • No graphic violence or disturbing content
  • No content that endangers minors
  • No promotion of self-harm or dangerous behavior
  • No content designed to incite hatred

No Spam or Manipulation

Don't use bots, fake accounts, or coordinated activity to artificially inflate engagement. Authentic interactions make Synvora better for everyone.

Protect Privacy

Don't share another person's private information without their consent. Respect the boundaries of private accounts.

Reporting

Use the ··· menu on any post or profile to report content that violates these guidelines. Our team reviews all reports promptly. Last updated: June 2026.
